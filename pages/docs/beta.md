# Beta notes

Version 2 of Talking Villagers is a **beta**. It is a complete rewrite of version 1 for
Minecraft 26.3, on Fabric and NeoForge.

- **Back up your worlds** before you try it.
- The voicepack format is version 2 and meant to stay. Packs you make now should keep
  working.
- Settings, events and screens may still change before the release.

## What is new

Everything, more or less: voicepacks as single files, the voicepack screen, the web
editor, wandering traders, witches and illagers, 67 events, child voices, languages per
village, commands and pack downloads on servers. The
[changelog](https://github.com/d4rkm0nkey/minecraft-talking-villagers/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md)
lists it all.

## Reporting bugs

Open an [issue](https://github.com/d4rkm0nkey/minecraft-talking-villagers/issues) with
your Minecraft version, loader (Fabric or NeoForge), mod version, what you did and the log
(`logs/latest.log`). For problems with a pack, attach the pack or the output of
`/tv packs list`.
